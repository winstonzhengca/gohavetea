Harmony, Not Sameness

和而不同

Harmony without sameness.

Poster transcript

Living together without becoming the same. Different words. Distinct histories. Shared attention.

Historical background

The classical phrase is well known from the Analects: “The exemplary person seeks harmony but not sameness.” The exhibition does not use it to romanticize consensus. Harmony requires work, and differences of power do not disappear because people share a table.

Curator’s interpretation

This phrase gives the exhibition an ethical horizon. Coexistence does not require assimilation. Difference should not be converted too quickly into hierarchy, suspicion, or stereotype. A shared cup is meaningful only when the people around it are allowed to remain different.

Artwork information

Ying Wen, 和而不同 / Harmony Without Sameness, calligraphy, 13 3/4 × 27 1/2 in., 2026, Edmonton.
