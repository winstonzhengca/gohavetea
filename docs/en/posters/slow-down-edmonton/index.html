Slow Down, Edmonton

With less, one gains; with more, one is bewildered.

少则得，多则惑 — Daodejing, chapter 22

Historical background

The line appears in a passage of the Daodejing that questions accumulation and fixed self-assertion. It should not be reduced to a modern productivity slogan or an instruction to own less.

Curator’s interpretation

At a crowded festival, “less” can mean making enough room to notice. The poster does not ask visitors to reject the city’s energy. It invites a different rhythm within it: fewer rushed conclusions, more attention to the leaf, cup, artwork, and person nearby.

Production correction Some draft artwork mislabels the chapter. The public website and final download must use Daodejing chapter 22.
