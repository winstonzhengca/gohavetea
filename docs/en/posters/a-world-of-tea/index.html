A World of Tea

Poster transcript

A world of tea. Many ways to share.

Curator’s interpretation

This horizontal postcard adapts the multilingual poster series to a portable object. Visitors can take it away, mail it, or use it as a small teaching prompt. Its portability continues the exhibition’s question: what changes when an image, word, or cup travels?

Go Have Tea — Tea Travels: Leaves, Care, and Everyday Invention. Curated by Junhong (Summer) Ma. Calligraphy by Ying (Joy) Wen. Paintings by Hui Yang.

A three-table exhibition for the 2026 Edmonton Heritage Festival. Website content package prepared July 2026.
