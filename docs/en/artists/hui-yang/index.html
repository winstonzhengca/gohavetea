Hui Yang

Hui Yang is the painter whose works provide many of the exhibition’s visual anchors. Her mountain landscape, teaware studies, floral still lifes, and other approved paintings bring attention to material form, movement, and dwelling.

In After Wang Meng’s Ge Zhichuan Moving His Dwelling, a classical composition is re-situated through a work made in Edmonton. The image of a family moving through mountains can be read not simply as retreat, but as reorientation: a search for rhythm, shelter, dignity, and renewed relation in a changing environment.

Within this exhibition, Yang’s work helps connect landscape with migration without claiming that one image can represent all immigrant experience. Her paintings also slow the viewer down. A cup, branch, flower, or rock becomes worthy of sustained looking.

Featured work

Hui Yang, After Wang Meng’s Ge Zhichuan Moving His Dwelling / 仿王蒙《葛稚川移居图》, 2022, acrylic on canvas, 24 × 60 in., Edmonton.
