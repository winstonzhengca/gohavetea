Wen Ying

Wen Ying contributes calligraphy to Go Have Tea. Her works Go Have Tea and Harmony, Not Sameness give the exhibition’s central phrases weight, spacing, gesture, and pace. The website should show the complete calligraphy before using any detail or crop.

Calligraphy is not decorative proof of cultural authenticity here. The shape, pressure, spacing, and rhythm of the brush make short phrases feel bodily and present—吃茶去 turns the exhibition’s central idea into an action, and 和而不同 makes coexistence without assimilation visible as a vertical sequence of inked characters.
