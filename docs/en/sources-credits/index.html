Image and quotation policy

Every image carries a nearby credit. Long quotations, community stories, portraits, recordings, maps, and photographs are reproduced only within their permissions. External links do not imply permission to copy material.

Educational and research notice

The wellness material shares cultural histories and community practices; it does not offer medical advice. Anonymous festival responses are not automatically research data. Any later academic use of identifiable visitor material requires a separate ethical and consent process.
