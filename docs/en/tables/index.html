Three Tables

The exhibition is arranged as three tables, but it tells one connected story. Each table begins with an action—transform, take root, or remix—and shows tea culture being made in practice.

There is no required order. Begin with the leaf, the plant, or the cup that interests you.

Chinese Tea — Transform

One plant, many processes, many traditions.

Wellness & Goji — Take root

Plants, family knowledge, migration, and care in Edmonton.

Bubble Tea — Remix

Taiwanese invention, global movement, customization, and labour.

Go Have Tea — Tea Travels: Leaves, Care, and Everyday Invention. Curated by Junhong (Summer) Ma. Calligraphy by Ying (Joy) Wen. Paintings by Hui Yang.

A three-table exhibition for the 2026 Edmonton Heritage Festival. Website content package prepared July 2026.
